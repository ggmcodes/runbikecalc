Gift ideas for bike riders: match the gift to the riding

The useful gifts for cyclists are the ones that make more riding possible: a smart trainer that keeps them fit through winter, a bike computer that gets them home from a new route, a power meter that turns "ride hard" into a plan. That is why this guide runs most expensive first. A serious cyclist gets more out of one Wahoo KICKR CORE 2 (Zwift Cog and Click) than out of a drawer of accessories, and the accessories are here too, further down, for the budget that suits.

Sizing the gift is easy: ask whether they ride indoors in winter (trainer), whether they ride new routes (computer), and whether they train with numbers (power meter). If they already have all three, skip to the mid-range and under $50 tiers, or use our gift finder, which uses their FTP to pick the right trainer tier.

The big gift: smart trainers, bike computers, power meters

Smart trainer first. For most cyclists in a cold climate, a direct-drive trainer is the single purchase that changes their year; it turns a dark evening into a structured hour with Zwift or TrainerRoad. The Wahoo KICKR CORE 2 (Zwift Cog and Click) is the value pick because it ships with the Zwift Cog and needs no cassette. The Wahoo KICKR V6 adds a heavier flywheel and more resistance for racers. The Garmin Tacx NEO 3M is the comfort pick for riders doing long indoor sessions.

Our smart trainer guide, power meter guide and bike computer guide compare every model in each category if you want the long version.

Then the computer. A mapping bike computer is the difference between riding the same loop and exploring. The Garmin Edge 1040 Solar is the flagship; the Garmin Edge 850 is the right size for most people.

Then power. A power meter is what lets a cyclist use our FTP calculator, power-to-weight calculator and training load calculator properly. Pedal-based meters like the Favero Assioma Duo and Garmin Rally RS210 are the easiest gift because they need no mechanic; just check which cleat system their shoes use (Look Keo for Assioma, Shimano SPD-SL for the Rally RS).

What to check before you buy

  • Cassette and drivetrain. The Wahoo KICKR V6 and Garmin Tacx NEO 3M need a cassette that matches their bike (speed and brand). The Wahoo KICKR CORE 2 (Zwift Cog and Click) does not, which is one reason it is our top pick as a gift. Carbon wheels need the right freehub.
  • Cleat system. Pedal power meters are cleat-specific: Look Keo for the Favero Assioma Duo, Shimano SPD-SL for the Garmin Rally RS210. Look at the bottom of their shoes.
  • Hitch size. The racks above are 2 inch hitch models; check the receiver on the car.
  • Sizes. Helmets, gloves and shoes are sized. Everything else on this page is one size.

Questions people ask before buying

What is the best gift for a cyclist?

For a cyclist who rides regularly, a smart trainer is the gift with the biggest impact, because it keeps them riding through winter. The Wahoo KICKR CORE 2 (Zwift Cog and Click) is our pick: it ships with the Zwift Cog so it needs no cassette, and it has WiFi like the more expensive Wahoo KICKR V6. If they already have a trainer, a mapping bike computer (Garmin Edge 850) or a pedal power meter (Favero Assioma Duo) is next.

What do you get a bike rider who has everything?

Look at the things a cyclist postpones: a Garmin Varia RTL515 radar rear radar, a Wahoo KICKR HEADWIND fan smart fan for the trainer, a proper Park Tool PCS-10.3 repair stand workstand, or a hitch rack like the Kuat Piston Pro X. Consumables also work: chamois cream, a cleaning kit, spare bottles. Our gift finder matches gifts to how they ride.

Smart trainer or power meter: which is the better gift?

If they ride outdoors a lot, the power meter, because it measures every ride and works with our FTP calculator. If their training is mostly indoors and structured, the trainer, because it controls resistance. For a rider with neither, the trainer is the bigger lifestyle change and the Wahoo KICKR CORE 2 (Zwift Cog and Click) is the safest choice.

Do I need to buy a cassette with a smart trainer?

With the Wahoo KICKR V6, Garmin Tacx NEO 3M and Garmin Tacx NEO 2T, yes: a cassette matched to their bike's speed and brand, plus fitting. The Wahoo KICKR CORE 2 (Zwift Cog and Click) ships with the Zwift Cog and Click, which replaces the cassette and works with any bike, so it needs nothing else.

What is a good gift for a new cyclist?

A floor pump (Topeak JoeBlow Sport III floor pump), a multi-tool (Crankbrothers M19 multi-tool), tyre levers, a rear light (Cateye ViZ450 rear light) and chamois cream (Chamois Butt'r Original (8 oz)) cover what a first-year rider does not own.
