Why training level decides the gift

Endurance gear is tiered by how much it is used. A flagship watch earns its price through multi-band GPS, maps and 30-hour battery; a beginner running twice a week on the same loop uses none of that, and the Garmin Forerunner 165 at a quarter of the price does everything they will touch. Flip it around and a marathoner running six days a week on a Garmin Forerunner 165 is missing training readiness, race prediction and a battery that survives a long race. The Garmin Forerunner 965 is a better gift for them than any accessory.

The same tiering applies on the bike. A rider who does not know their FTP has no use for a Garmin Tacx NEO 3M; a Saris M2 wheel-on smart trainer wheel-on trainer at a tenth of the price keeps them riding through winter, and if they take to it, next year's gift is the Wahoo KICKR CORE 2 (Zwift Cog and Click). A rider who trains to power and races gets full value from the Wahoo KICKR V6, and a dual-sided Garmin Rally RS210 or a set of Shimano Ultegra C50 carbon wheelset wheels is the upgrade they have been postponing.

Questions people ask before buying

How do I know what level of runner or cyclist someone is?

Ask how many sessions they do a week, or check a recent race result. One or two sessions is a beginner; three or four with occasional races is consistent; five or more with a goal race is serious; anyone who can tell you their FTP, heart-rate zones or VO2 max is data-driven. Our race time predictor and FTP calculator turn a single number into a tier.

What is the best gift for a runner who has everything?

Recovery gear and accuracy: Hyperice Normatec Elite boots, a Theragun Prime (6th Gen), a Polar H10 chest strap. A runner who has every gadget rarely has good recovery tools, and a chest strap upgrades the watch they already own.

What is the best gift for a cyclist who has everything?

The add-ons they keep postponing: a Wahoo KICKR HEADWIND fan fan, a Wahoo KICKR CLIMB, a Garmin Varia RTL515 radar radar, a Park Tool PCS-10.3 repair stand workstand, or a hitch rack like the Kuat Piston Pro X. Consumables (Chamois Butt'r Original (8 oz), Park Tool GP-2 patch kit) always land.

Is a Forerunner 165 enough for a beginner runner?

Yes. The Garmin Forerunner 165 shows pace, distance and heart rate and offers daily suggested workouts, which is everything a beginner uses in their first year. Move up to the Garmin Forerunner 265 once they start racing regularly, and the Garmin Forerunner 965 when they train for a marathon.

Should I buy a smart trainer for a new cyclist?

A wheel-on trainer like the Saris M2 wheel-on smart trainer is the right first trainer: under $200, works with any bike, and it keeps a new habit alive through winter. The direct-drive Wahoo KICKR CORE 2 (Zwift Cog and Click) is the gift once they have stuck with it for a season.
