What it does well

  • Integrated lights and a licence plate holder are not a luxury. In many places a rack that obscures the vehicle's lights or plate is illegal, and buying a rack that solves that is cheaper than the alternative.
  • Nine point eight inches between bikes is generous and it is the specification that stops handlebars and pedals gouging the frame beside them.
  • Telescopic arms with pivoting cradles clamp the frame or wheel of almost any bike shape, including step through and full suspension frames that defeat simpler racks.
  • Tool free AutoAttach means fitting and removing the rack does not involve a spanner in a car park in the rain.

Where it falls short

  • Seventeen hundred dollars is a great deal of money for a bike rack, and a two bike rack from the same brand or from Kuat costs far less.
  • Three bikes plus a heavy rack is a lot of tongue weight, and you must check your vehicle's hitch rating rather than assuming.
  • A rack this long behind the car changes how it parks and reverses, and it will catch on steep driveways.
  • Even folded it is a large object to store, and it is heavy enough that fitting it is a two person job for many people.

What owners report

Published owner reports praise the loading mechanism and the lighting, and describe it as noticeably better engineered than cheaper racks. The bike spacing draws specific praise from owners carrying bikes with wide bars.

The criticism is price and weight. Reports repeatedly mention checking hitch tongue weight ratings before buying, which is genuinely important with a rack this substantial and three bikes on it.

Who it suits

  • Families and groups regularly carrying three bikes.

Who should skip it

  • Two bike households, where the same engineering costs far less.

Common questions

Do I need lights on a bike rack?

It depends where you drive, and it is worth checking rather than assuming. A rack that obscures your vehicle's tail lights or licence plate is illegal in many jurisdictions, and enforcement varies. A rack with integrated lighting and a plate holder removes the question.

What hitch do I need?

This fits a 2 inch receiver. More importantly, check your vehicle's tongue weight rating against the weight of the rack plus three bikes, particularly with ebikes, which can weigh 60 lb each. Exceeding the rating is a genuine safety issue rather than a technicality.

Will it fit my bike?

Telescopic arms with pivoting cradles handle most frame shapes including step through and full suspension. Very long wheelbase bikes, fat bikes and some cargo bikes need checking against Thule's compatibility information. The extra long wheel holders help with long wheelbase bikes.

Hitch rack or roof rack?

Hitch racks are far easier to load, do not affect fuel consumption as badly and do not risk driving into a low garage. Roof racks keep the boot accessible and suit vehicles without a hitch. For heavy ebikes a hitch rack is the only sensible option. Our best hitch bike racks roundup covers the range.

Can I still open the boot?

Most quality hitch racks tilt down to allow access, and this one folds and tilts. With three loaded bikes the tilt takes effort, which is worth knowing if you access the boot frequently on a trip.
